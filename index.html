Competitive Exams

Must-Master competitive exams.

GATE

For M.Tech, Ph.D. admissions, and PSU jobs in India.

GRE

For MS, Ph.D. admissions, and scholarships abroad (mainly USA, Canada, Europe).

CAT

For MBA admission in India (IIMs and other top B-schools).

IELTS

For studying, working, or migrating to English-speaking countries like the UK, Canada, and Australia.

GMAT

For MBA, Business Master's abroad, and top B-schools admissions.

XAT

For MBA and management programs in India, especially XLRI and other top B-schools.

TOEFL

For admission to English-speaking universities and institutions abroad, especially in the USA, Canada, and other countries.

MAT

For MBA/PGDM admissions in Indian B-Schools.
